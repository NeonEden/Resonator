## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an Obsidian Futurism aesthetic: an uncompromising collision of heavy mid-century laboratory instrumentation, brutalist industrial hardware, and high-energy particle physics simulation. The interface is not merely a software representation; it functions as a physical, rack-mounted synthesizer chassis machined from blackened, anisotropic brushed metal, equipped with copper bezels, weathered steel continuous encoders, and incandescent vacuum readouts.

Targeted at sound designers, modular synthesists, avant-garde electronic musicians, and audiovisual engineers, the environment evokes mechanical weight, tactile authority, and hazardous electrical potency. Interactions demand deliberate precision: rotary controls offer haptic resistance, toggle switches snap with mechanical authority, and patch points seat with metallic finality. The visual language blends cold militarized laboratory telemetry with the raw organic luminescence of exposed vacuum chambers, plasma containment conduits, and glowing filament cathodes.

## Colors

The color architecture is divided into two distinct physical tiers: Structural Material Substrates and Luminous Energy Emitters.

### Structural Substrates (Physical Chassis)
- **Blackened Chassis (`#121416`):** The master structural foundation. A light-absorbing, cold-rolled blackened metal field used across viewport backgrounds, base rack slots, and unpopulated modular bays.
- **Graphite Plates (`#292C2D`):** Individual Eurorack modular faceplates and panel segments. Provides separation from the master chassis with subtle surface variations.
- **Weathered Steel (`#81766B`):** Machined rotary dial bodies, knurled thumb screws, slide potentiometers, and structural brackets.
- **Copper Bezels & Jacks (`#AD7655`):** Exposed conductive contact points, 3.5mm patch cable socket rings, grounded chassis fasteners, and terminal nuts.
- **Raw Umber Earth (`#765440`):** Secondary structural accents, heat sinks, calibration dividers, and module classification stripes.
- **Screen-Printed Ivory (`#E7E0D3`):** High-durability, industrial stenciled typography and silkscreen iconography applied directly to faceplates.

### Luminous Emitters (Plasma & Filament)
- **Fire Amber (`#FFB545`):** Primary signal indicator, master control active states, primary patch line highlight, and calibrated peak telemetry.
- **Mineral Cyan (`#59CBD0`):** High-frequency CV modulation, gate triggers, sub-oscillator monitoring, and secondary signal routings.
- **Blazing Flame (`#FF682C`):** Overdrive saturation, hard clipping indicators, tertiary routing matrix nodes, and active hazard thresholds.
- **Deep Blue (`#244577`):** Unengaged signal buses, ambient reactive glow, low-voltage ground states, and chassis shadow depth.
- **Hot Filament (`#FFE58A`):** Vacuum tube core highlights, ultra-high voltage arcs, cursor contact points, and momentary trigger flashes.

## Typography

Typography functions as durable industrial screen printing applied to matte graphite and metal plates. All text elements are rendered in Ivory (`#E7E0D3`) or raw emission hues, treated with uppercase styling for structural tags, control titles, and telemetry indicators.

- **Primary Headings (`Space Grotesk`):** Reserved for system modules, rack unit designations, and top-tier functional headers. Sharp, technical geometric architecture with mechanical cuts.
- **Readout & Body Text (`Space Mono`):** Used for oscilloscope telemetry, frequency readings, parameter value outputs, and system settings. Retains consistent terminal widths to avoid layout shifts during real-time value changes.
- **Component Silk-Screens & Labels (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to individual potentiometer tags, I/O jack designations, calibration scales, and hardware parameter markers. High track-spacing ensures total legibility at small scale within dense 3U Eurorack faceplates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an analog Eurorack standard (Horizontal Pitch [HP] and Unit [U] grid modules). A single horizontal rack tier represents a standard 3U enclosure height, sub-divided into consistent vertical HP units (multiples of 5.08mm / 0.25rem increments).

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views utilize horizontal scrolling or multi-tier rack stacking. Each module snaps strictly into 4HP, 8HP, 12HP, 16HP, or 24HP width columns. Gutters between module panels represent true physical panel seams (`space-xs`).
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Multi-tier full Eurorack canvas. Modules arrange horizontally across 84HP to 126HP bounds per rail with unobstructed dynamic Bezier cable patching.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Two-tier rack configuration. Modules auto-wrap or scroll along horizontal rails; controls switch to expanded tap targets.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-tier vertical rack stack. Cable routing transforms into an addressable node/matrix patch list to prevent screen occlusion while preserving panel interactions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through machined physical stratification, mechanical beveling, and luminous photon dispersion rather than standard drop shadows.

1. **Sub-Chassis Tier (-1):** Socket barrels, slide potentiometer trenches, and terminal cutouts recessed into panel surfaces. Rendered via top-weighted inner shadows (`inset 0 3px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.85)`) and dark borders (`#121416`).
2. **Faceplate Tier (0):** Flat graphite plates (`#292C2D`) defined by crisp, 1px perimeter chamfers using `#3D4143` on the upper and left edges, and `#181A1B` on the lower and right edges to simulate directional down-lit workshop illumination.
3. **Hardware Surface Tier (+1):** Weathered steel knobs, copper jacks, and metal lever caps. Cast dense, localized contact drop shadows (`0 4px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.7)`, `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.9)`).
4. **Active Emission Tier (+2):** Plasma reactor displays, filament indicator lamps, and active patch cords. Elevated above all mechanical surfaces via dual-stage chromatic bloom: a focused core emission (`0 0 6px currentcolor`) layered over a soft field dissipation (`0 0 18px currentcolor`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a strictly sharp silhouette geometry (`0` roundedness). Modules, toggle plates, oscilloscope screens, numeric readouts, and panel dividers are cut with raw, 90-degree industrial edges to evoke milled plate metal and extruded aluminum racks. 

Radial exceptions are strictly reserved for physical functional hardware: perfectly circular circular jack bores, knurled cylindrical knobs, and socket retaining hex nuts. Buttons simulate stamped sheet-metal blocks or square mechanical keys with micro-chamfered 1px borders, avoiding soft or organic corner radii.

## Components

### Knobs & Rotary Encoders
- **Body:** Weathered Steel (`#81766B`) machined circular face with directional radial-brushed gradients and tactile outer knurling rings.
- **Indicator:** Inset marker line filled with Screen-Printed Ivory (`#E7E0D3`) or Fire Amber (`#FFB545`) when parameter is modulated.
- **State:** Hover increases bezel specularity; active drag triggers an orbital trace in Cyan (`#59CBD0`) or Amber (`#FFB545`) with numeric tooltip readouts in `JetBrains Mono`.

### Jack Sockets & Patch Cables
- **Sockets:** Concentric multi-tiered rings: outer hexagonal fastener in Raw Umber (`#765440`), inner contact cylinder in Copper (`#AD7655`), and central bore in Blackened Chassis (`#121416`).
- **Cables:** Semi-rigid physics-simulated Bezier splines. Unpatched cables hang dynamically; active lines utilize saturated braided-cloth textures tinted to Fire Amber, Mineral Cyan, or Blazing Flame, complete with cast drop shadows spanning underlying faceplates.

### Toggle Switches & Mechanical Latches
- **Mount:** Hexagonal threaded copper retaining collar.
- **Actuator:** Brushed chrome/steel bat lever with physical up/down tilt orientation.
- **Feedback:** Audio-tactile mechanical snap on activation, paired with an adjacent 3mm flush LED filament indicator.

### Push Buttons & Step Triggers
- **Form:** Low-profile rectangular or square stamped-metal keys.
- **Unpressed:** Surface flush with bezel, delineated by a 1px border (`#81766B`), filled with `#1C1E20`.
- **Pressed:** Inset translation of 1px down and right, illuminated by back-lit Hot Filament (`#FFE58A`) or Fire Amber (`#FFB545`).

### Numeric Readouts & Oscilloscopes
- **Display Frame:** Recessed rectangular lens tinted with Deep Blue glass (`rgba(36, 69, 119, 0.2)`).
- **Telemetry:** Continuous phosphor or plasma waveforms rendered in Mineral Cyan (`#59CBD0`) with variable trace-decay persistence and high-contrast monospace data streams.

### Module Faceplates & Cards
- **Construction:** Graphite Rack Plate (`#292C2D`) with corner mounting holes showing threaded copper machine screws.
- **Silk-Screening:** Functional section dividers marked with 1px solid or dashed Ivory lines (`#E7E0D3` at 40% opacity), complete with industrial high-voltage warning symbols, signal path directional arrows, and module I/O taxonomy.
